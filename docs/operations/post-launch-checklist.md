# Post-launch checklist

At 15 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours, and 24 hours review uptime/readiness, p95 latency, 5xx, checkout conversion, payment/webhook status, queue failures, reservations, seller fulfillment, refunds, payouts, reconciliation exceptions, DB/Redis capacity, security alerts, and backup age.

Record release SHA, migration version, dashboards checked, incidents, customer impact, financial exceptions, and follow-up owners. Keep the release under heightened monitoring until two consecutive reconciliation and backup checks are clean.
